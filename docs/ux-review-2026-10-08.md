# UX review of Oct 8, 2026: the ten findings and where they stand

## How the review was done

An AI reviewer drove Chrome on macOS with real clicks and key presses, and read
no code. Page zoom was fixed at 90%, so the screenshot text was 10% smaller than
at 100%. The window was sized until the page reported 1440 x 900 and
1280 x 720. Chrome would not go narrower than 500 px, so the phone pass used a
390 x 844 frame, without touch and without a mobile browser. In the pass with
the reviewer's own prompt, every pasted output was written by the reviewer, not
by a model.

The state below is after the fixes of Oct 8, checked on the built app at
100% zoom at 1440 x 900, 1280 x 720 and in a 390 px frame, on the example path
and on an own-prompt path with pasted outputs. No real phone, touch device or
screen reader was used.

## The ten findings

| # | Finding | State | What changed, and what is left |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outputs are unreadable on a short laptop screen | Fixed | Each output has a reading area of at least 320 px and the page scrolls when the window is too short. Measured: 483 px at 1440 x 900 and 320 px at 1280 x 720, before and after rating. At 1280 x 720 the page scrolls by about 30 to 40 px. |
| 2 | Editing an input deletes outputs and ratings without asking | Fixed | Steps 2 and 3 say how many outputs and ratings continuing will remove, the button names it, and "Undo my change" puts the fields back. The "Use sample workflow" question names what it leads to removing. The removal rule is unchanged. |
| 3 | On a phone the comparison is not side by side and half can be skipped | Fixed in the 390 px frame | The model switch is in the rating bar and shows each model's rating or "not rated". Next input goes to the top and says once if the other model is unrated. The input text is collapsed. Not tried on a real phone. |
| 4 | The result and decision open with ratings missing | Fixed | The result screen lists the inputs that still need a rating, each with a button back to it. The three decision choices appear only when every output that was tested has a rating. An output that was not tested does not hold them back. |
| 5 | Status label names the wrong model | Fixed | "Not rated yet" and "Not tested" no longer name a model. "Better", "Same rating" and "Worse" keep "Compared model:". |
| 6 | The example drops you at step 5 and never shows the prompt | Partly | "Show the prompt" is on the rating screen, closed. The example still opens on step 5, and the steps it filled read "from the example" instead of "done". |
| 7 | My own comparison has a band of "not measured" | Fixed | With nothing measured, each model says so once, one line gives the reason, and the note about a single run is not shown. |
| 8 | The rating screen spends its space on everything but the outputs | Fixed | Three columns, a source line shown once for both models, no repeated cost line, equal panel headers. Small gold labels are a darker gold at 5.36:1 on white (4.80:1 on the cream panels), and no label is under 12 px. |
| 9 | Keyboard focus stays behind after "Next input" | Fixed | Focus moves to the heading of the first output, and the focus ring is 2 px in ink. |
| 10 | Only four models, already chosen | Partly | Step 2 now says that the four models listed are the ones Switch Check has prices for today. The lists still start on two chosen models, as before. |

## Not covered by these fixes

Rendering the formatting marks in outputs, blind rating, saving or sharing, and
the run path were left alone on purpose.
